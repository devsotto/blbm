Balamban Liempo

Tastier than lechon — herb-stuffed pork belly, charcoal-roasted to a crackling golden brown.

Lechon, as famous as it is in Cebu, certainly is the most-sought after food among locals and tourists. With “Tastier than Lechon” as tagline, BALAMBAN LIEMPO, without doubt, bravely declares this. It is a rightful claim, warranted by very tender succulent meat with flavorful and aromatic herbs and other secret green ingredients.

Its crackling, golden brown skin paves the way to intermingled layers of delicious melt-in-your-mouth fat and luscious meat — and the claim is backed up by the recognition as SunStar Best of Cebu’s Best Liempo. Truly, Cebu’s best liempo is taking Cebu by storm.
