How it works

Three steps, no queue.

Ordering is branch-based, so the closest pit to you does the roasting and hands it over ready to eat.

  1. Pick a channel

    Use a delivery app, message the official Facebook page, or call your branch directly — whichever is fastest where you are.

  2. Set your address

    Your order is routed to the branch that covers your area. If you are unsure which one that is, message us and we will point you.

  3. Roasted to order

    The belly is stuffed with herbs and roasted over charcoal when you order — not held under a lamp — so it arrives crackling.

Good to know

Cut-off, fees & coverage.

Delivery terms are set per branch and per platform, so the exact figures live with your order rather than on this page. Here is what to expect before you check out.

  • Coverage Depends on the branch serving your address. Over 90 branches across Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao — check the branch directory to see who is near you.
  • Cut-off time Set by your branch and by the delivery app. Roasting takes time, so ordering before the evening rush gets you a hotter, crispier delivery.
  • Delivery fee Charged by the delivery platform and shown in-app before you confirm. Fees vary with distance and demand.
  • Payment Follows whatever your chosen channel supports — cash on delivery or the app’s own cashless options.
