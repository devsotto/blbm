A six-time SunStar Best of Cebu brand, ninety-plus outlets nationwide, and a product people queue for. Here is what an outlet typically costs to put up and what a single branch can look like once it is running.

Read this first — illustrative figures Every peso figure and projection on this page is a planning illustration for layout and discussion. It is not an offer, a guarantee, or a financial commitment. Official franchise fees, the audited ROI model and the signed terms are released in the franchise packet after site screening.
1 — The package

What a franchise covers

Brand & trade dress

Use of the Balamban Liempo name, logo, menu boards and store design system.

Herb supply

The native green stuffing blend, sourced and specified the Cebuano way.

Roasting training

Charcoal pit setup, stuffing technique, portioning and food safety.

Operations manual

Recipes, SOPs, opening and closing checklists, service standards.

Launch support

Opening campaign, community marketing and social page assets.

Ongoing supply

Approved suppliers, refresher training and field visits.
