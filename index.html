Nganong kami?

Herbs. Size. Price.

Three reasons people keep talking about it long after the plate is cleared.

See what's new
  1. 01

    Herbs

    Stacked with the unique and special herbs used in authentic Cebu lechon. A distinct flavor people talk about long after they have tasted the recipe.

  2. 02

    Size

    600g uncooked, which cooks down to a full 1/2 kilo served. Most take-out liempos put out 250–300g. Ours is not a snack.

  3. 03

    Price

    Average lechon runs ₱375/kilo in Cebu and about ₱600 in Metro Manila. Measured by the kilo, Balamban Liempo comes in better priced — gram per gram, other liempos cost more.
