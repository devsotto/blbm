About Us

A taste
of history.

From its humble beginnings, the concept has never changed. Our goal was simple: to provide a high-quality, unique and flavourful experience when eating pork belly — without it, the brand suffers and quality deteriorates.

Balamban Liempo Food Corner was established in July 2009 by Jojo and Honey Vergara. Honey grew up in Abucayan, in the Cebu town of Balamban — a farm and fishing municipality known for a native method of grilling liempo with local herbs and secret ingredients. Inspired by that distinct concept, the couple set up their first take-out shop at the corner of Tres Borces and Cabahug streets, Mabolo, Cebu City.

What started as a small, stand-alone shop is now a fast-expanding enterprise with branches across the country. Wandering clients became regulars, the word spread, and our customers just keep coming back for more.

Company Mission

Why we keep
the pit lit.

To introduce to Filipinos the flavourful Balamban style of roasting liempo (pork belly) — the unique taste of lechon Cebu, its local herbs and spices, and a more convenient and cost-effective way of tasting liempo, ultimately making a truly unique and gourmet liempo experience.

To achieve, within ten years, the recognition of Balamban Liempo as an all-time Filipino favourite, available to Filipinos all over the world.

Getting started

How to franchise

Five steps from your first e-mail to the keys of your very own store.

  1. 01

    Send your letter of intent

    E-mail balambanliempocebu@gmail.com with the subject “Balamban Liempo Franchise” and attach your resume and biodata.

  2. 02

    Submit your location

    Send a picture or sketch of where the store will stand. Standard sizes: 10–12 sq m for a take-out store, 20–40 sq m for stores with dine-in.

  3. 03

    Feasibility check

    We send an officer to check the area. A 1 km radius jurisdiction makes sure no other Balamban Liempo is selling inside your coverage.

  4. 04

    Review the contract

    If your area passes, we provide a franchise contract for you to review and discuss at your own pace.

  5. 05

    Build & turn over

    Agree on the terms, sign and settle the franchise fee. We build the store in 30 days, launch it and turn over the keys to your very own Balamban Liempo.
